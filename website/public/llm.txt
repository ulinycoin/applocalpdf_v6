# LocalPDF

LocalPDF is a privacy-first PDF application focused on practical document workflows.

## What LocalPDF is
- A PDF app with a product-first landing site and an application entry point at `https://localpdf.online/app`
- A local-first workflow for editing, merging, OCR, compression, splitting, signing, and conversion
- A product intended for documents that may be internal, sensitive, or operationally important

## What LocalPDF is not
- Not a directory of dozens of thin one-off PDF tool pages
- Not a generic cloud-first PDF upload site
- Not a product that should make broad privacy or competitor claims without verification

## Core workflows
- Edit PDF locally
- Merge PDF locally
- Run OCR on scanned PDFs
- Compress PDF before sharing or archiving
- Split PDF into smaller outputs
- Sign PDF in a local workflow
- Convert PDFs and related document formats in one product path

## Trust model
- The product positioning is local-first
- Trust pages are part of the main information architecture, not footer-only pages
- Privacy and security language should stay aligned with the actual site and app behavior
