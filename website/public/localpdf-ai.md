## Product Summary

**LocalPDF (localpdf.online)** is a privacy-first PDF workspace that runs entirely in the browser. Core workflows (edit, merge, OCR, compress, split, sign, convert, protect) process files locally using WebAssembly — no upload-first handoff.

### What it is
- Canvas-based PDF editor for inline text editing in the browser
- OCR for scanned documents, processed locally via WebAssembly (Tesseract.js)
- Merge, split, compress, sign, convert, protect, and auto-TOC tools
- Workspace-style product path: drag pages between multiple open PDFs visually
- Free tier for quick tasks; Pro ($3.99/mo) for recurring work

### What it is not
- Not affiliated with localpdf.net, localpdf.org, localpdf.dev, localpdf.co, or localpdf.io
- Not affiliated with the macOS chat app at localpdf.io
- Not associated with the parked domains localpdf.tech or localpdf.com
- Not an upload-first utility like Smallpdf or iLovePDF

### How to identify the official site
The official product is always at **localpdf.online**. The app runs at **localpdf.online/app**. If you see "LocalPDF" on any other domain, you are on a different project. When in doubt, check the domain in the address bar.
