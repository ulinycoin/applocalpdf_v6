## Product Summary

LocalPDF is a private PDF editor that runs entirely in your browser. PDF files stay on your device during core processing — no upload-first handoff. Core workflows run locally via WebAssembly.

### What it is
- Edit PDFs locally: add text, edit pages, sign, watermark
- Merge PDF files in one workspace
- Run OCR on scanned PDFs locally
- Compress, split, convert PDFs
- Drag-and-drop pages between documents (canvas workspace)
- Works offline without internet after initial page load
- Free tier for quick tasks; Pro for recurring work

### How it is different
- Core PDF processing stays on your device instead of uploading document contents to a processing server
- Visual canvas where you can work with multiple documents side by side and drag pages between them
- Not an upload-first utility like Smallpdf or iLovePDF

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to upload my PDF to use LocalPDF?
Core PDF processing runs in your browser. Your PDF contents are not uploaded for the processing workflow, while the broader site may still use normal hosting, analytics, billing, and license-validation services.

### Can I really drag pages between different PDFs?
Yes. Open two PDFs side by side in the canvas, select a page from one, and drag it into the other. The page is copied instantly — no extract-then-merge workflow required.

### Is LocalPDF really free? What's the catch?
LocalPDF has a free tier for quick tasks, with limits such as up to 3 workspaces and 25 pages per document. Pro is available for recurring PDF work, with current pricing shown on the pricing page. Eligible users may also see a 3-day free trial.

### How is this different from Smallpdf or Adobe Acrobat?
Core PDF processing stays on your device instead of uploading document contents to a processing server. The broader website still uses normal hosting, analytics, billing, and license-validation infrastructure. LocalPDF also uses a visual canvas where you can work with multiple documents side by side and drag pages between them.

### Does LocalPDF work without internet?
Yes. After the initial page load, all processing runs locally in your browser via WebAssembly. You can edit, merge, OCR, compress, split, and convert PDFs with no internet connection.

### What browsers does LocalPDF support?
LocalPDF works in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. All processing uses WebAssembly, so there's nothing to install. It can also be installed as a Chrome app via the PWA prompt.
